

# OpenWhispr

[](https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr#openwhispr)

The open-source and free alternative to WisprFlow and Granola.  
Privacy-first voice-to-text dictation with AI agents, meeting transcription, and notes. Cross-platform for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr/tree/main/openwhispr-mobile

The Expo mobile application lives in [`openwhispr-mobile`](https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr/blob/main/openwhispr-mobile) with its own dependencies, lockfile, build configuration, and release process.
See the [mobile README](https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr/blob/main/openwhispr-mobile/README.md) for its setup instructions.

# Nemotron Bubble

[](https://github.com/spf13/nemotron-bubble#nemotron-bubble)

**A tiny, beautiful push-to-talk dictation bubble for Windows.**

Press your shortcut, speak, and your words appear at the cursor — powered by **on-device** NVIDIA Nemotron speech recognition.
